STRENGTH · INCOME

Your total yearly income, all sources. Paid monthly? Monthly × 12 = yearly.

DEXTERITY · LIQUIDITY

If your income stopped today, how many months could your savings cover your bills? Count only full months.

CONSTITUTION · NET WORTH

What you own minus what you owe. Build it below — or skip the workshop if you already know your number. Own a home? List its full value as an asset and its mortgage as a debt — the math finds your equity for you.

INTELLIGENCE · CREDIT

Your credit score (300–850). Don't know it? Most banking apps show it free.

WISDOM · DEBT DISCIPLINE

Add up your monthly debt payments. Divide by your monthly income. That's your DTI — example: $600 ÷ $3,000 = 20%.

CHARISMA · NETWORK

Count the people you could ask for a professional favor — a referral, an introduction, a connection to money — and reasonably expect a yes. Not followers. Not contacts. People who would answer.
